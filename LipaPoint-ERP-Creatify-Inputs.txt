PRODUCT NAME: LipaPoint ERP

PRODUCT URL: https://lipapoint.co.ke

CATEGORY: All-in-one ERP / business management software for Kenyan SMEs

TARGET MARKET: Kenya

LANGUAGE: Kenyan English

VIDEO LENGTH: 30 seconds (plus 15s and 6s cutdowns)

ASPECT RATIO: 9:16 vertical master; 1:1 and 16:9 variants

SHORT DESCRIPTION: LipaPoint ERP is an all-in-one business management system for Kenyan businesses. It brings point of sale, M-Pesa and card payments, inventory, purchasing, staff and statutory payroll, accounting and KRA eTIMS invoicing into one system - so an owner can see sales, stock, cash and profit without rebuilding the numbers in a spreadsheet.

LONG DESCRIPTION: LipaPoint ERP runs the whole business from one login. On the counter it handles cash, M-Pesa (including STK Push) and card payments, split payments and receipts, and it keeps working when the internet drops. Behind the counter it tracks stock per location, flags reorders and expiries, and records transfers and stock-takes. On the finance side it keeps the general ledger, bank reconciliation and financial statements in step with every sale, while KRA eTIMS invoices are issued from the same transaction. Staff records, scheduling and payroll - PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and housing levy - post into the same books. Owners get daily summaries and reports by hour, item, sales rep, payment method and cashier, plus an AI assistant. Plans start at KSh 999 a month for a single-till retail shop, with no setup fees and no lock-in.

PRIMARY AUDIENCE: Kenyan owner-operators of growing retail SMEs - independent mini-supermarkets, hardware stores, pharmacies and general shops with 2 to 5 tills, 3 to 25 staff and one to three branches. Typically 30 to 45 years old, hands-on, running KSh 800,000 to 5,000,000 in monthly sales. They already use a basic till or POS, Excel or a notebook, WhatsApp for supplier orders, and pay a bookkeeper monthly. They are the person who locks up, counts the cash and still answers stock questions on a Sunday.

SECONDARY AUDIENCES: Restaurant and bar owners (food cost, waste, shift reconciliation); pharmacy owners (expiry losses, stock control, eTIMS); growing wholesalers and distributors (multi-warehouse stock, supplier accounts, cash tied up in dead lines).

AUDIENCE TO EXCLUDE: Large chains and manufacturers already running SAP, Oracle or Microsoft Dynamics; single-table kiosks with no staff and no compliance exposure; NGOs and public institutions with long procurement cycles.

PAIN 1: Closing the day takes one to two hours of matching the till, the M-Pesa messages and the cash drawer - and it still does not balance.

PAIN 2: Stock is invisible: fast movers run out, dead stock ties up cash, and shrinkage only shows up at stock-take.

PAIN 3: KRA eTIMS and VAT paperwork is manual, delayed and worrying - invoices live in a separate system from sales.

PAIN 4: The books are rebuilt every month from three or four sources, so the owner never knows true margin per branch or product.

PAIN 5: Discounts, voids and price overrides leave no trail, so cash shortages and staff accountability are guesswork.

PAIN 6: With more than one branch, the owner drives between shops to compare them because there is no single view.

PAIN 7: Statutory payroll - PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, housing levy - is a monthly scramble done in a spreadsheet.

DESIRED OUTCOME: An owner finishes the day with certainty instead of paperwork: one screen that already knows what sold on each till, what came in by M-Pesa, what stock is low and what the day actually earned. No reconciling, no spreadsheet.

KEY BENEFITS: Every till, M-Pesa payment and cash movement reconciled in one daily summary; stock, reorders, expiries and transfers visible before they become losses; KRA eTIMS invoices issued from the same sale on Enterprise plans; one set of books that payroll, purchases and sales all post into; cashier, discount and void trails that make shortages visible; offline selling with sync when the connection returns.
